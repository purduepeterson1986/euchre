Euchre

You and your computer partner against two computer players. First team to 10 points wins.

Players

Names are optional. Your partner sits across from you; the other team sits on your left and right.

Game speed

How long the computer players take over each bid and card.

House rules

In the trump suit the jack is the highest card (the right bower). The other jack of the same colour is second highest (the left bower) and counts as a trump card. You must follow the suit that was led if you can.
