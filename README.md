# Euchre

Euchre for one player. You and a computer partner play against two computer players, and the first team to 10 points wins.

**Play it here:** https://purduepeterson1986.github.io/euchre/

It runs in any modern browser on a computer, tablet or phone. There is nothing to install.

## How to play

Euchre uses 24 cards: the 9, 10, jack, queen, king and ace of each suit. Everyone gets five cards and one card is turned face up.

**Naming trump.** Starting on the dealer's left, each player may tell the dealer to pick up the turned-up card, which makes its suit trump, or pass. If all four pass, the card is turned down and each player may name a different suit or pass.

**The bowers.** The jack of the trump suit is the highest card (the right bower). The other jack of the same colour is the second highest (the left bower) and counts as a trump card, not as a card of its own suit. Then come the ace, king, queen, ten and nine of trump. The game marks the trump cards in your hand with a gold edge and labels the two bowers.

**Playing.** The player on the dealer's left leads. You must follow the suit that was led if you can; if you can't, you may play any card. The highest trump wins the trick, or the highest card of the suit led if nobody played trump. The winner of a trick leads the next one. Cards you are not allowed to play are dimmed.

**Scoring.** The team that named trump needs at least three of the five tricks.

| Result | Points |
|---|---|
| Named trump and took 3 or 4 tricks | 1 |
| Named trump and took all 5 tricks | 2 |
| Went alone and took all 5 tricks | 4 |
| Named trump and took fewer than 3 (a euchre) | 2 to the other team |

## House rules

- **First to 10 points** wins the game.
- **Stick the dealer.** If everyone passes in both rounds, the dealer has to name trump, so every hand gets played.
- **Going alone.** Whoever names trump may play the hand without their partner. Switch on **Go alone** before you make your call. If your partner goes alone, you sit that hand out.

## The computer players

All three computer players use the same method. For every bid and every card, they deal out the cards they can't see in many different ways that fit what has happened so far: which cards have been played, who has run out of a suit, and who bid or passed. They work out the best play of each of those deals and choose the bid or card that wins the game most often. They can't see anyone's cards, including their partner's.

They also take the score into account. For example, they bid more boldly when the other team is one point from winning.

## Controls

- Click or tap a card to play it. With a keyboard, use Tab to move between cards and buttons, and Enter to choose.
- **Restart** starts a new game. **Settings** goes back to the first screen, where you can name the players and choose the game speed.
- Sound can be switched off with the speaker button, and there is a full-screen button beside it.

The game remembers your settings and how many games you have won.

## Running it from your own computer

Download `index.html`, `styles.css`, `engine.js` and `game.js` into one folder and double-click `index.html`. The game needs no internet connection once you have the files.

## How it is built

Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. There are no frameworks, no build step, and no image or sound files: the cards are drawn with CSS and the sounds are generated by the browser.

- `engine.js` holds the rules of euchre and the computer players.
- `game.js` draws the table and runs the game.

When `styles.css`, `engine.js` or `game.js` changes, change the `?v=` tag on all three lines in `index.html` so browsers fetch the new copies.
